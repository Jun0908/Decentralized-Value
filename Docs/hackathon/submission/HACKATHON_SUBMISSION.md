## Exact boundaries

- The continuation reconstructs an existing purchased prefix from records. It does not repurchase the deliverable or pay twice.
- The Commander is limited to three continuation actions; the simulator advances the remaining time. Full autonomous investigation, repeated purchases, patching and resumption were not demonstrated.
- Alongside real analysis, the Commander receives the simulator's public report. Estimated severity/classification is uncertain modeled information, not hidden ground truth.
- Protocol operations, loss denominated in USD and game credits are simulation. The Sepolia token payment is a real transaction. Pool allocations are previews.
- This is one public Episode, not an analysis-masked control study or an unknown Final. Information-value improvement and resistance to over-practice are unproven.
- Context: `rescue-purchased-analysis-continuation-v1`. Earlier Practice/payment evidence is not overwritten.
- Public JSON summarizes the recorded execution. A separate public replay fixture supplies Episode, Playbook, purchase action, decision sequence and expected outcomes so anyone can recalculate simulation/Pool results without secrets. Private runtime journals remain ignored. Public replay is not cryptographic proof of OpenAI execution or diagnostic truth.
